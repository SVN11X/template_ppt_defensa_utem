## UTEM rules on the degree exam

- **Reglamento General de los Estudiantes de Pregrado**, consolidated text, Resolución Exenta N° 0607 of 18 March 2025. https://transparencia.utem.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/RES607DE2025.pdf. Título XII (articles 65 to 68) lists the ways to graduate and leaves the protocol of each one to the program's own regulation. The announcement of the grade must be public. The final grade combines the study plan average with the grade of the degree work.
- **Reglamento Plan de Estudios, Ingeniería Civil en Ciencia de Datos** (Anexo E, Vicerrectoría Académica). https://transparencia.utem.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Reglamento-Ingenieria-Civil-en-Ciencias-de-Datos.pdf. The degree exam is the public defense of the thesis work. The commission has at least three members: the head of the program, the guide professor and two specialists. The student presents for at most 30 minutes, then answers questions. The commission grades with a rubric set in advance, without the student in the room. The exam weighs 40 to 50 percent of the degree work grade. This is one program: rules change from program to program.
- Other program regulations are in the same folder of the UTEM transparency site, for example https://transparencia.utem.cl/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Reglamento-Ingenieria-en-Informatica.pdf.
- No public rubric for the defense was found. Students should ask their school for it.

## The owner's own defense

The owner defended in the Escuela de Electrónica of the Facultad de Ingeniería, in person, with a limit under 20 minutes set by the school. The commission asked mainly why that solution and not another, and about the limitations. The owner's lessons: rehearse more times with a clock, and use simpler language. The slide catalog in `templates/` reproduces the design of that defense with the content removed.
